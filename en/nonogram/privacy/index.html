Pixel Nonogram Privacy Policy

Developer: ChauVB (chauvb.dev) · Effective November 13, 2026

This policy explains how the Pixel Nonogram game (Android, application ID com.chauvb.nonogram) collects, uses and shares information. By using Pixel Nonogram, you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

Pixel Nonogram has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress (solved levels, the game in progress, remaining hints and magic wands) and preferences (light/dark theme, tutorials seen, rating prompt) are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android version of Pixel Nonogram shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads (which you choose to watch to get an extra hint or magic wand) and interstitial ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Your advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and operating system information (model, language, version);
  • Ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and diagnostic data.

This data is used to serve ads, cap their frequency, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, if you consent, personalize ads. Google handles it under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open the app, you are asked whether your data may be used for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy.
  • On any Android device: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings (usually Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads).
  • The game works offline, and no ads are loaded while you are offline.

4. In-app purchases

You can buy the "Play without ads" pack (one-time payment). Purchases are processed by Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details. The game asks Google Play whether you own the pack (so it is restored when you reinstall) and stores the result on your device.

Daily check-in: each time the game starts it sends a small request to a Google server to get the current time, used to work out check-in days and when today-only uses expire. The request carries nothing about you beyond what any network connection reveals (such as your IP address). Your check-in history is stored only on your device.

5. Rating the app

The game may ask you to rate it from 1 to 5 stars. Your choice is stored only on your device, so you are not asked again, and it is never sent to us. If you choose 5 stars, the game opens the Pixel Nonogram page on Google Play. Reviews you post on Google Play are governed by Google's policies.

6. Children

Pixel Nonogram is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can deal with it.

7. Sharing and security

We do not sell personal data, and we do not collect any data beyond what is described above. Advertising data is collected directly by Google and sent over encrypted connections.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions, email: chauvb.dev@gmail.com
